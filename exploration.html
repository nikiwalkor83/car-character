Historical Context

Why did cars change?

Cars did not change for one reason. Regulations, fuel costs, technology, manufacturing, consumer preferences, and economic conditions all shaped what manufacturers could and wanted to build. This context helps frame the patterns shown by the vehicle data; it does not claim that one factor caused a specific change unless the evidence directly supports that conclusion.
